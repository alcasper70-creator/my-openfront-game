Free beta service

The current build is free and experimental. Matches, accounts, settings, and availability may change or be reset without notice. The operator may restrict access where needed to keep the game safe and playable.

Accounts and purchases

The current public build supports guest play only. It does not sell virtual items, process payments, or promise a persistent inventory. If cloud accounts, leaderboards, or a store are introduced later, this page will be updated before those features go live.
