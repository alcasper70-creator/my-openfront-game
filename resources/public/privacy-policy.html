Frontline Frenzy — Privacy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy describes the current guest-play beta. It will be updated before Google sign-in, cloud profiles, leaderboards, payments, or advertising are enabled.

What the game stores

Your browser stores local game preferences, a guest player identifier, and any name you choose to use. This lets the game reconnect you in that browser. Clearing browser site data removes those local values.

What the game server receives

When you connect, the game host necessarily receives connection and gameplay information such as your network address, guest identifier, player name, lobby activity, and match actions. Hosting providers may retain technical logs to run and secure the service.

Current feature limits

This version has no Google sign-in, no cloud account database, no public cloud leaderboard, no payment processing, and no official social-media integration. If Firebase services are enabled in a later version, the data collected, purpose, retention, and contact route will be listed here first.

Your choices

Use a nickname rather than personal information in your player name. You can clear the game's local browser data at any time. Do not put private or sensitive information into lobby names or chat-like fields.

Project contact

Before public launch, the project owner must publish a support contact and legal operator details here. The public source is available at github.com/alcasper70-creator/my-openfront-game.
